The Hexomel Story

Hexomel was born from a passion for preserving Portugal's rich beekeeping heritage. Our name combines "Hexagon" - representing the perfect geometry of honeycomb - with "Mel" - the Portuguese word for honey.

Our Mission

At Hexomel, we believe that honey is more than just a sweetener - it's a connection to nature, tradition, and the hard work of thousands of bees.

We work exclusively with local Portuguese beekeepers who share our commitment to sustainable practices and bee welfare. Every jar of Hexomel honey is a tribute to their expertise and dedication.

Our honey is never heated, filtered, or processed - just pure, raw honey as nature intended.

Our Values

Purity

We never add anything to our honey. What you get is 100% pure, raw honey straight from the hive.

Bee Welfare

Our beekeepers prioritize the health and happiness of their bees, using sustainable and ethical practices.

🌍 Sustainability

We're committed to protecting the environment and supporting biodiversity through responsible beekeeping.

🏆 Quality

Every batch undergoes rigorous quality testing to ensure the highest standards.

🤝 Community

We support local beekeepers and their families, preserving traditional knowledge.

📦 Transparency

We're open about our sourcing, processing, and business practices.

The Heroes Behind Our Honey

Honeybees are remarkable creatures. A single bee visits up to 5,000 flowers in a day, and it takes the lifetime work of 12 bees to produce just one teaspoon of honey. When you enjoy Hexomel honey, you're tasting the dedication of thousands of bees and the pristine Portuguese landscapes they call home.
